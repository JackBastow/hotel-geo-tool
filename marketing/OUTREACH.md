# First 10 customers: outreach you can send today

Goal: 10 Founding Hotels paying £49.99 a month. That is about £500 a month for 12 months, and 10 real reports to learn from and turn into case studies (with permission).

## Who to ask first (easiest to hardest)
1. Hotels you already know: former colleagues, suppliers' contacts, GMs you've met. A personal message beats any ad.
2. Independent and small-group hotels (1 to 30 rooms to 100 rooms) where the owner or GM reads their own email. They have no marketing team and feel the OTA commission most.
3. Small groups of 3 to 10 hotels: bigger prize (Portfolio at £79 a hotel), but a slower decision.

Skip big chains at the start. They have procurement, legal and long cycles.

## The offer in one line
"I'll show you what ChatGPT-style assistants say when someone asks where to stay near you, who they recommend instead, and the first things to fix. Free one-page snapshot first."

The free snapshot is your hook: it costs you about 10 minutes of the tool and gives them something real about their own hotel. Send it by hand, then offer Founding access.

## Message 1: email or LinkedIn (short, personal)
Subject: What AI tells guests about [Hotel name]

Hi [Name],

More guests now ask an AI assistant where to stay. I ran a quick check for [Hotel name] in [town]: when someone asks for [a specific thing, e.g. "a hotel near the station with parking"], [it named / it did not name] you, and it named [rival] instead.

I've put it on one page, with the first two or three things I'd fix. Happy to send it over, free, no catch. Just reply "yes".

[Your name]
presencepro.co.uk

(Run the snapshot before you send, so the first message already contains one real finding. A message with a real finding about their hotel gets replies; a generic pitch doesn't.)

## Message 2: after you send the snapshot (two days later)
Hi [Name], did the one-pager make sense? If it's useful, I'm taking a small first group of hotels at £49.99 a month for the first 12 months (normally £99), in return for feedback on the reports. Details and a short form here: presencepro.co.uk/founding.html. No pressure either way.

## When they say yes
1. They fill the form at /founding.html (it emails you).
2. In Stripe: Product catalog, Coupons, "Founding Hotel offer", Promotion codes, New. Limit to one redemption. Email them the code and the link: presencepro.co.uk/founding.html (the "Continue to checkout" link).
3. After they pay, run their audit and email the report. Ask for their review export.

## Rules to keep
- Don't promise AI will recommend them or that bookings will rise. Say what the report shows.
- Say plainly it is one assistant on one day.
- Don't claim customers or results you don't have yet.
- Don't send the same message in bulk. Ten personal messages a day for a week beats 500 generic ones, and bulk email to people who haven't asked can breach UK email-marketing rules.
- Use a separate sending identity from your employer (hello@presencepro.co.uk), and mind your employment contract.
