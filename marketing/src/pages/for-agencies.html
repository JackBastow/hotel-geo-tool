Questions

Common questions

Is AI visibility something a hotel marketing agency can offer clients?

Yes. Hotel guests increasingly ask AI assistants where to stay, and most hotels have no way to see whether AI recommends them or a competitor. Tracking that and turning it into action steps sits naturally alongside SEO and reporting.

Does Presence Pro replace SEO?

No. It sits alongside it. Clear, accurate pages help both, but AI assistants also quote booking sites, reviews and press, and their answers vary, so it is a separate thing to measure.

How can an agency try it?

Ask for a free one-page snapshot for one client, or a hotel you are pitching. You see the output before deciding anything.

Can an agency guarantee results to a client?

No, and we would advise against promising any. The AI assistants do not publish how they choose. What an agency can show is the gaps, the actions taken and the progress over time.
