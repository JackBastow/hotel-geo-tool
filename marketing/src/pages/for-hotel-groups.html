Questions

Common questions

Can a hotel group track AI visibility across all its hotels?

Yes. Presence Pro's Portfolio plan gives a group one login for every hotel, with analytics on how each hotel is doing, how many actions are done and which hotels need attention.

Does each hotel in the group see the other hotels?

No. Each hotel's own dashboard shows only that hotel. The group sees all of its hotels in the portfolio dashboard.

How many hotels does the Portfolio plan cover?

Portfolio is for groups of 5 to 49 hotels, priced per hotel, with a lower price per hotel in the larger tier. Groups with 50 or more hotels are quoted individually.

Can we start with one hotel?

Yes. Request a free one-page snapshot for one of your hotels to see what AI can see about it today, then decide.
