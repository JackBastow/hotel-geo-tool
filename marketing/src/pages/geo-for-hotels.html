GEO (generative engine optimisation) is the work of making a hotel easy for AI assistants to find, understand and recommend: accurate facts on its own pages, listings that agree, credible mentions elsewhere, and regular checking of what assistants actually say.

What a hotel controls

Six things you can actually do.

Most of GEO is unglamorous, and all of it is in your hands.

1. Facts on your own pages

Parking, breakfast times, check-in, cancellation and distances, written as plain text on a page a guest would open.

2. Agreement

The same name, address, phone number and policies wherever they appear. Two check-in times give an assistant a reason to be wrong.

3. Listings

Complete, correct profiles on maps, booking sites and directories, saying what your website says.

4. Reviews

Specific, kind replies to critical reviews. Never bought or incentivised reviews.

5. Credible mentions

Honest inclusion in local guides, tourism pages and articles that name hotels in your area.

6. Access

Not blocking, by accident, the crawlers you want reading your site. See which ones.

GEO and SEO

Related, not the same.

The goalRank a page in a list of results.Be named in an assistant's written answer.

SourcesMostly your own pages and links to them.Your pages, booking sites, reviews, maps, guides and press, all in one answer.
ConsistencyA ranking is fairly stable day to day.The answer can differ each time the question is asked.
How you measure itPosition and traffic.How often you are named across repeated questions, who is named instead, and which pages were cited.

What GEO can't do.

We'd rather say it plainly than have you find out later.

  • Guarantee a recommendation. The assistants don't publish how they choose, and they change often.
  • Prove cause and effect. If an assistant starts naming you after a fix, you can't be sure the fix caused it.
  • Work through tricks. Hidden text, fake reviews and paid placement dressed as editorial can get a hotel into trouble.

Questions

Common questions

What is GEO for hotels?

GEO, or generative engine optimisation, is the work of making a hotel easy for AI assistants to find, understand and recommend: accurate facts on its own pages, consistent listings, credible mentions elsewhere, and regular checking of what assistants say.

Is GEO the same as SEO?

No, though they overlap. SEO aims to rank pages in a list of search results. GEO aims to be named in an assistant's written answer, which draws on several sources and varies from run to run.

What other names does GEO go by?

You will also see answer engine optimisation (AEO), AI SEO and LLM optimisation. The terms overlap and are used differently by different people.

Does GEO guarantee that AI recommends a hotel?

No. The assistants do not publish how they choose and they change often. GEO removes gaps and errors that would otherwise leave a hotel out, but it cannot guarantee a recommendation.
