GEO for hotels: what it means, and what to do.

Generative engine optimisation is how a hotel gets found, understood and recommended by AI assistants. Here is what that involves in practice.

Updated {{ updated }} · 5 minute read

The short version

  • GEO (generative engine optimisation) is the work of making a hotel easy for AI assistants to find, understand and recommend.
  • It overlaps with SEO but is not the same: the goal is to be named in a written answer, not to rank in a list.
  • Most of it is unglamorous: accurate facts on your own pages, listings that agree, credible mentions, and checking regularly.
  • It cannot guarantee a recommendation, and anyone who says it can is guessing.

What GEO cannot do

  • Guarantee a recommendation. The assistants do not publish how they choose, and they change often.
  • Prove cause and effect. If an assistant starts naming you after you fix something, you cannot be sure the fix caused it.
  • Work through tricks. Hidden text, fake reviews and paid placement dressed as editorial are the kinds of things that get a hotel into trouble.

How to know whether it is working

Ask the same realistic guest questions every month and write down whether you were named, who was named instead and which pages were cited. Compare like with like, and ignore small changes, because answers vary. You can do this by hand with five questions in ten minutes: the test is in our guide to how assistants choose hotels. Doing it properly, for more questions and with the evidence kept, is what AI visibility tracking is for.

Common questions

What is GEO for hotels?

GEO, or generative engine optimisation, is the work of making a hotel easy for AI assistants to find, understand and recommend: accurate facts on its own pages, consistent listings, credible mentions elsewhere, and regular checking of what assistants say.

Is GEO the same as SEO?

No, though they overlap. SEO aims to rank pages in a list of search results. GEO aims to be named in an assistant's written answer, which draws on several sources and varies from run to run.

What other names does GEO go by?

You will also see answer engine optimisation (AEO), AI SEO and LLM optimisation. The terms overlap and are used differently by different people.

Does GEO guarantee that AI recommends a hotel?

No. The assistants do not publish how they choose and they change often. GEO removes gaps and errors that would otherwise leave a hotel out, but it cannot guarantee a recommendation.
