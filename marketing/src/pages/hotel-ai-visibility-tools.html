Common questions

Can I check my hotel's AI visibility for free?

Yes. You can ask an AI assistant five realistic guest questions about your area and record who is named and which pages are cited. Some hotel-technology companies also offer free checkers. A one-off check is a first look, because answers vary from run to run.

What should a hotel AI visibility tool do?

It should ask questions the way guests do without naming your hotel, repeat each question, show which pages were cited, separate your own website from third-party sources, give actions with evidence, say clearly when something could not be assessed, and keep history so changes can be compared.

Is a hotel-specific tool better than a general AI visibility tool?

It depends. General tools are built for brands in any industry and can track many prompts. Hotel-specific tools can ask hotel questions, such as parking, breakfast and distance to landmarks, and check hotel sources such as booking sites. Compare what each one does with your own questions.
