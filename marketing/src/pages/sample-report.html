3. What to do first

The actions most worth doing, in order. Each shows its evidence. Confirmed means a quotation checked on a page the audit read.

1. Say whether a parking space can be reserved, on your Parking page

Confirmed
Found
The Parking page states a price but not whether a space can be reserved.
Evidence
"Parking is available at £18 per night."
Page: /parking
Why it matters
"Best hotel with free parking" was one of the questions where the hotel was named in only 1 of 3 answers.
Do this
On the existing Parking page, state whether spaces can be booked in advance, how, and whether any are free for longer stays.

2. Two pages give different check-in times

Confirmed
Found
Check-in is 15:00 on one page and 14:00 on another.
Evidence
"Check-in from 15:00"
/rooms
"Arrive from 14:00"
/faq
Do this
Decide the real time and make both pages, and your listings, say the same.

3. Answer the critical reviews that have no reply

Likely
Found
From your own review export: 11 of the 38 reviews rated 2 stars or below have no reply.
Do this
Reply to each, newest first, using the skeleton reply provided as a starting point. Never offer anything in return.

4. A city guide the assistant cited lists rivals but not you

Worth checking
Found
An editorial "where to stay" article was cited in 4 answers. It names three rival hotels and not yours.
Do this
Use the article's own contact page to introduce the hotel, if it suits you. Do not pay for placement presented as editorial.

5. Add a line about early breakfast to the airport-stay page

Likely
Found
The hotel serves breakfast from 6:30, but only on the Dining page. The "early flight" page doesn't mention it.
Do this
Add the breakfast time and a link to the Dining page, so the answer sits where a traveller would look.
