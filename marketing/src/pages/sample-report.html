3. A missed question, followed to an action

The part of the report that matters most: where an assistant recommended rivals instead, what it relied on, and what the hotel can do.

Business travellers near the conference centre

  1. The question"Which hotels near the conference centre suit a business trip?"
  2. VisibilityThe Harbour Lane Hotel was named in 0 of 5 answers.
  3. CompetitionMarlow & Finch Hotel was named in 4 of 5, and Cathedral Close Inn in 5 of 5. Both are comparable: four-star, city centre.
  4. EvidenceThe assistant cited a business-travel guide (a 2025 article listing hotels near the centre) and the conference centre's own "where to stay" page. Neither mentions the Harbour Lane.
  5. OpportunityYour website doesn't name the conference centre or give a distance to it, and your meeting rooms are only on a PDF. Worth checking
  6. ActionAdd the walking distance and your meeting facilities as text on a page guests would open. Then use each page's own contact route to ask for a listing. Don't pay for placement presented as editorial.
  7. MeasurementThe same question is asked again next month. The dashboard shows the change, and says "too early to say" unless the ranges clearly differ.

4. The opportunities, ranked

Ranked by how much each is likely to help the hotel be found and recommended. Each shows its evidence. Confirmed means a quotation checked on a page the audit read.

1. Get onto the pages the assistant relied on

Worth checking
Found
An editorial "where to stay" article was cited in 4 answers, and the conference centre's own page in 3. Both name two comparable rivals and not yours.
Why it matters
These are the pages the assistant used to build its answer. Your rivals are on them; you are not, so the assistant has nothing to quote about you.
Do this
Use each page's own contact route to introduce the hotel, if it suits you. Do not pay for placement presented as editorial.

2. Become findable for the conference-centre questions

Likely
Found
"Hotels near the conference centre" named the hotel in 0 of 5 answers; two comparable rivals were named in 4 and 5. Your website doesn't name the centre or give a distance to it, and your meeting rooms are only on a PDF.
Why it matters
The hotel has meeting rooms, so this is a question it can credibly answer, and it is where the gap with rivals is widest.
Do this
Add the walking distance and your meeting facilities as text on a page guests would open.

3. Close the gap where rivals are named and you are not

Worth checking
Found
0 of 3 answers for "family-friendly hotel near the old town", and 0 of 2 for "hotels with meeting rooms near the station". The assistant named other hotels for each.
Why it matters
These are the questions guests ask when they are choosing between hotels.
Do this
Check that the Rooms and Meetings pages say, in text, whether family rooms and meeting facilities are offered, and how far the hotel is from the old town and the station.

What this could mean commercially

Each missed question is a guest choosing between hotels without seeing yours. Where the assistant quotes a booking site instead, a booking there costs commission. We can't predict how many bookings an assistant sends. Put your own numbers into the break-even calculator.
