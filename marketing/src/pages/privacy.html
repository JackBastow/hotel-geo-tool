Privacy and cookies

Last updated {{ updated }}. Written in plain English.

Who we are

Presence Pro decides how and why your personal information is used, and is the “controller” under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). {{ business }} You can contact us about anything in this notice at {{ contact.email }}.

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies, has no analytics or advertising trackers, and loads nothing from other companies' servers when you read it.
  • We collect only what we need to answer you, run your subscription and prepare your reports.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete it at any time.

What we collect, why, and our legal basis

If you contact us (form or email)

What: your name, email address, hotel website, the topic and your message. Why: to reply and to help you. Basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries; or, if you are about to subscribe, steps you ask us to take before a contract. Kept: for up to 12 months after the last message, unless you become a customer.

If you apply to be a Founding Hotel

What: your name, email address, role, your hotel's name, town or city and website, and anything you write in the application. Why: to review your application and reply. Basis: our legitimate interest in responding to the request you made. Kept: for up to 12 months if you are not approved; if you become a customer it is kept with your account.

If you request a free snapshot

What: your name, email address, and your hotel's name, town or city and website. Why: to prepare your snapshot, email it to you, and send one follow-up asking whether it was useful. Basis: our legitimate interest in responding to the request you made. Kept: for up to 12 months, unless you become a customer.

If you subscribe

What: your name, email address, billing details, hotel name, town or city and website, the plan you chose and your payment status, and the notes you give us about your hotel. Stripe takes the payment and holds your card details; we do not. Why: to run your subscription, prepare and send your reports, and keep accounting records. Basis: performing our contract with you, and our legal obligation to keep financial records. Kept: while you subscribe and for up to 24 months afterwards, then deleted or anonymised; invoicing and accounting records for six years, as tax law requires.

Information about your hotel in reports

Reports are built from your hotel's public website and other public sources, so they mostly contain business information, not personal data. Where public pages name individuals (for example a general manager), a report may mention them as the page does. Basis: our contract with you and our legitimate interest in preparing it. We keep reports as described above.

If you give us a review export

A review file may contain personal data about guests. We ask you not to include contact details. Our analysis does not use reviewer names. The review text is analysed to prepare your report and is not saved; only counts and rates (for example how many critical reviews have no reply) are kept. For this data you are the controller and we are your processor, as the terms of service explain.

If you use the chat assistant

The questions you type are sent to our server and then to an AI provider (Anthropic) so that a reply can be written. We do not store the conversation. We keep only counters (how many replies we have given, and an estimated cost) and, briefly in memory, your internet address so we can limit how many messages one visitor can send. Please do not put personal or confidential information into the chat. Basis: our legitimate interest in answering questions about our service.

When you visit this website

Pages are served by GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub receives your internet address and browser details when your browser asks for a page, and handles them under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not receive that information from GitHub and do not use it.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website sets no cookies and uses no local storage for tracking, so there is no cookie banner. If you click through to Stripe to pay or manage billing, Stripe's pages are run by Stripe and set their own cookies; Stripe explains these on its pages.

Who else handles your information

We use these providers to run the service. They handle information on our behalf, or in the case of Stripe as an independent controller for payments:

  • Stripe: card payments and billing.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.
  • Render and Neon: host the application and database that prepare reports and the chat assistant.
  • Anthropic: the AI used for the chat assistant, for sampling AI answers, and for reading hotel pages in reports.
  • Microsoft: email for the messages you send us and we send you.
  • Web3Forms: delivers the messages you send through the forms on this website (the contact form and the hotel details form) to our email.
  • Search and data services, such as Tavily, Google and OpenStreetMap: they receive public information such as your hotel's name and website address as search terms.

We may also share information where the law requires, or with advisers under a duty of confidence.

Sending information outside the UK

Some of these providers are based in, or process information in, countries outside the UK, including the United States. Where that happens we rely on safeguards the law recognises, such as the UK's adequacy regulations for the UK-US data bridge, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or the provider's equivalent commitments.

Keeping it safe

We use encrypted connections, restrict access to the people who need it, and choose providers with recognised security practices. No online service is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting you occurred we would tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office as the law requires.

Your rights

You have the right to: be told what we hold about you and get a copy; have inaccurate information corrected; have it deleted in many cases; restrict or object to our using it, including where we rely on legitimate interest; and receive the information you gave us in a portable format where we use it under a contract. To use any of these, email {{ contact.email }}. We will answer within one month and will not charge unless a request is clearly excessive.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

Presence Pro is for businesses, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this notice

If we change what we collect or how we use it in a way that matters to you, we will update this page and, for customers, tell you by email.
