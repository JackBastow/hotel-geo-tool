AI visibility tracking for hotels: what to track, and why.

Guests now ask AI assistants where to stay. AI visibility is whether your hotel is one of the hotels named, and what to do when it isn't.

Updated {{ updated }} · 5 minute read

The short version

  • AI visibility is whether AI assistants name your hotel when a guest asks where to stay, how often, who they name instead, and which pages they used.
  • It is a different measure from search ranking. An assistant writes one answer from several sources, and that answer changes from run to run.
  • One check proves little. Tracking means asking the same questions repeatedly, recording the sources, and comparing month to month.
  • Nobody can guarantee a recommendation. You can fix the gaps an assistant would otherwise fill from somewhere else.

What AI visibility means for a hotel

When a guest asks an assistant "Which hotel near the conference centre is best for a business trip?", it names a few hotels and often shows the pages it used. Your hotel is either one of them or it isn't. Tracking AI visibility means measuring that, for the questions your guests really ask about your area.

What to do with it

  1. Fix the facts you control. Parking, breakfast times, check-in, distance to landmarks and cancellation terms, written as text on a page a guest would open.
  2. Make listings agree. Booking sites, maps and directories should say what your website says.
  3. Find the pages that name your rivals and not you. Local guides and "where to stay" pages. Seek honest inclusion, never paid placement dressed as editorial.
  4. Check again next month with the same questions, and look for changes that are larger than the normal variation.

What nobody can promise

The assistants do not publish how they decide, and they change often. We can show the gaps and the progress; we cannot promise that fixing them makes an assistant recommend a hotel. For the full picture of what is known and not known, read how AI assistants choose which hotels to recommend.

Common questions

What is AI visibility for a hotel?

It is whether AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude name your hotel when a guest asks where to stay, how often, who they name instead, and which web pages they rely on to decide.

Is AI visibility the same as SEO?

They overlap, because clear and accurate pages help both. But assistants also quote booking sites, reviews and press, and their answers vary from run to run, so tracking it means asking the same questions repeatedly and recording the sources, not watching one ranking.

Can anyone guarantee that AI will recommend my hotel?

No. The assistants do not publish how they choose, and answers change. What a hotel can do is fix the facts on its own pages, keep listings consistent and track the results over time.

How often should a hotel check its AI visibility?

Monthly is a sensible rhythm for most hotels, using the same questions each time so that changes are comparable. A single check proves little because answers vary.
