Questions

Straight answers, including the limits

Will this get AI assistants to recommend my hotel?

We can't promise that, and nobody honestly can. Presence Pro shows what an assistant said, which sources it relied on and what your own pages leave unanswered, so you can fix real gaps.

Which AI assistants do you test?

Today the sampled answers come from one assistant with live web search, labelled as a sample of one assistant on one day. For ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and others the report gives you a checklist to run by hand. We'd rather say so than pretend to cover them.

Where does the data come from?

Your public website, open sources such as OpenStreetMap and Wikidata, public search results, and the AI assistant's answers. We don't read Booking.com or Tripadvisor directly, as their terms prohibit it; you can upload your own review export for a full review analysis.

What are the limits?

AI answers vary, so we show a range and never a ranking. Anything we couldn't measure is marked "not assessed", never scored as zero. We can't prove why an assistant chose a hotel, or that one action caused a change. Read the methodology.

How do I get my reports?

Subscribe and tell us your hotel's website. We prepare your report, email it, then repeat every month with the change since the last one. There's nothing to install.

What happens to my data?

Reports go only to you. Review text from an export is analysed for that run only and not saved. See the privacy notice.
