Honest by design

A thin score dressed up as certainty is worse than no score.

Hotels will act on this, so Presence Pro is built to be right, and to say when it can't be sure.

The score always shows what it rests on

"72, across 60% of the full model." Anything we couldn't measure is marked not assessed, never scored as zero and never quietly left out.

Not found is not the same as missing

If we read some of your pages and found nothing, we say not found on the pages checked. A page we couldn't open is never counted against you.

AI answers are a labelled sample

One assistant, on one day, asked more than once. We never turn it into a market ranking, and we never claim to know why an assistant chose a hotel.

We respect other sites' rules

Booking and review platforms prohibit automated reading, so we don't do it. Your own review export gives you the full analysis instead, and the review text is never saved.

No promises we can't keep

No guaranteed rankings and no "become #1 on ChatGPT". Advice never involves fake or incentivised reviews, paid placement passed off as editorial, or posting as a guest.

Every recommendation shows its evidence

What we found, the page or quotation behind it, why it matters, exactly what to do and where. A recommendation with no evidence is withheld.

Questions

Straight answers

Will this get AI assistants to recommend my hotel?

We can't promise that and nobody honestly can. What Presence Pro does is show what an assistant said, which sources it relied on, and what your own pages leave unanswered, so you can fix real gaps. It never promises rankings.

Which AI assistants do you test?

Today the sampled answers come from one assistant with live web search, and the report labels them clearly as a sample of one assistant on one day. For ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and others the report gives you a checklist you can run by hand. We would rather be clear about that than pretend to cover them.

Do you read Booking.com and Tripadvisor?

Not directly. Their terms prohibit automated collection and we respect that. We identify your listings through public search results, and you can upload your own review export (a CSV or Excel file from any platform) for a full review and reply analysis.

How do I get my reports?

You subscribe and tell us your hotel's website. We prepare your report and email it to you, then again every month, with the change since the last one. There's nothing to install and no tool to learn.

What happens to my data?

Your reports are sent only to you. We keep the results so your hotel can be tracked from month to month, and we never show them to anyone else. Review text from an export is analysed for that run only and is not saved; only counts and rates are kept. See the privacy notice.

Does it work outside the UK?

Yes. It works for any hotel with a website, and searches are run as if from the hotel's own country. A few checks, such as the UK food hygiene rating, apply only where that data exists, and say so when they don't.
