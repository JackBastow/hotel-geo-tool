Questions

Straight answers

Will this get AI assistants to recommend my hotel?

We can't promise that, and nobody honestly can. Presence Pro shows what AI said, which sources it relied on and what your own pages leave unanswered, so you can fix real gaps.

Where does the data come from?

Your public website, open sources such as OpenStreetMap and Wikidata, public search results, and AI search answers. We don't read Booking.com or Tripadvisor directly, as their terms prohibit it; you can upload your own review export for a full review analysis. Read the methodology.

How do I get my reports and dashboard?

Subscribe and tell us your hotel's website. We prepare your report, email it, then repeat every month with the change since the last one. There's nothing to install.
