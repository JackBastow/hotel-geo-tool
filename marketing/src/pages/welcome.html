Thank you

You're in. Here's what happens next.

Your payment went through Stripe, and a receipt is on its way to your inbox. Three quick things, and your reports begin.

  1. Check your emailStripe sends your receipt, with a link to manage or cancel your subscription any time. If it doesn't arrive in a few minutes, look in your spam folder.
  2. Confirm your hotel detailsAt checkout you gave us your hotel's name, town or city and website. Use the form below to confirm them, add any other hotels on your plan, and tell us anything you'd like us to focus on or any questions of your own you'd like tested.
  3. Send your review exportExport your reviews from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Google or Expedia as a CSV or Excel file and email it to us. It is analysed for that report only. The text isn't saved, and reviewer names are never read.

Your hotel details
