Thank you

You're in. Here's what happens next.

Your payment went through Stripe, and a receipt is on its way to your inbox. Three quick things, and your reports begin.

  1. Check your emailStripe sends your receipt, with a link to manage or cancel your subscription any time. If it doesn't arrive in a few minutes, look in your spam folder.
  2. Confirm your hotelYou gave us the hotel's name and website at checkout. Reply to your receipt email, or write to {{ contact.email }}, to tell us the town, anything you want us to focus on, and, on Professional and Portfolio plans, up to five questions of your own.
  3. Send your review export (Professional and Portfolio)Export your reviews from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Google or Expedia as a CSV or Excel file and email it to us. It is analysed for that report only. The text isn't saved, and reviewer names are never read.
