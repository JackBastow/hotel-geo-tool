How a subscription works

From payment to your first report

1. Subscribe

Choose a plan and pay by card through Stripe. At checkout you give us your hotel's name, town or city and website so we know exactly what to report on.

2. We set you up

We confirm your hotel and the markets it competes in, and ask for your review export.

3. Reports every month

Each month you receive a new report by email, with the change since the last one and a plan of what to do first. Portfolio customers also get their dashboards.

Billing questions

The small print, in plain English

How do I pay?

By debit or credit card, through Stripe. Stripe handles the payment securely and sends you a receipt. Presence Pro never sees or stores your card number.

Is VAT included?

No. Every price on this site is before VAT. VAT is added at checkout where it applies, and appears on your invoice.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Cancelling stops the next payment and your plan runs to the end of the period you've already paid for. There's no minimum term. Full terms are in our terms of service.

What's the difference between monthly and yearly?

Yearly billing is available on Single Hotel at {{ price_single_annual }} per hotel, paid once upfront. That saves {{ price_single_saving }} a year compared with twelve monthly payments of {{ price_single }}. Portfolio is billed monthly; for an annual portfolio contract, ask for a quotation. Reports are the same either way.

How does Portfolio pricing work?

Portfolio is for groups of 5 to 49 hotels. For 5 to 19 hotels each hotel is {{ price_portfolio }} a month; for 20 to 49 hotels each hotel is {{ price_portfolio_large }} a month. The tier you fall into sets the price for every hotel on the account, not just the extra ones. You get a portfolio dashboard with trends across all your hotels, and each hotel also gets its own dashboard that shows only that hotel. To add or remove hotels later, email us.

Why does Portfolio cost less per hotel than Single Hotel?

It is volume pricing: the more hotels on one account, the lower the price per hotel. Portfolio also includes more than Single Hotel: the dashboards and ongoing analysis across the group.

Do I need to prove how many hotels I have?

Portfolio pricing is for the hotels you own or manage. We may ask you to confirm the list, and if the number on your account does not match the hotels you actually have, we'll adjust the plan. If you want a monthly report for only a few hotels, Single Hotel can be bought for as many hotels as you like.

What if I have 50 or more hotels?

Enterprise is a custom quotation. It can include options such as portfolio-wide reporting, centralised dashboards, cross-property benchmarking, consolidated billing, custom reporting, additional account support and tailored implementation arrangements, which we agree with you. Contact us.

What is the Founding Hotel programme?

A limited number of selected hotels can join as early partners at {{ price_founding }} a month for their first {{ founding_months }} months, and help shape the platform through feedback. It is by application and approval, not automatic. After {{ founding_months }} months the subscription moves to the standard price in force at the time, and we tell you in advance. See the details and how to apply.

Does Presence Pro include consulting or hands-on help?

Presence Pro is primarily software and monthly reports: what AI says about your hotel, why, and what to fix first. It does not include unlimited consulting or hands-on implementation. Advisory support may be offered separately later.

Can I buy Single Hotel for more than one hotel?

Yes. Choose how many hotels at checkout, and each gets its own monthly report. If you have 5 or more hotels and want the portfolio dashboard and a dashboard for each hotel, Portfolio costs less per hotel.

Can I change plan?

Yes. Email us and we'll move you between plans. Existing subscriptions keep the price they started on until we tell you otherwise, with notice in advance.

Will the reports guarantee AI recommends my hotel?

No, and no honest provider can promise that. The report shows what an AI assistant said, where it got its information and what your own pages leave unanswered, so you can fix real gaps. The AI answers are a labelled sample of one assistant on one day.

What if I want to see one first?

Read the sample report, or ask us any question about what your hotel's report would cover.

I need an invoice or have a billing problem

Stripe emails a receipt with every payment. For an invoice in your company's name or anything else about billing, email {{ contact.email }}.
