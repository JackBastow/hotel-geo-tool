How a subscription works

From payment to your first report

1. Subscribe

Choose a plan and pay by card through Stripe. At checkout you give us your hotel's name, town or city and website so we know exactly what to report on.

2. We set you up

We confirm your hotel and the markets it competes in, and ask for your review export.

3. Reports every month

Each month you receive a new report by email, with the change since the last one and a plan of what to do first. Portfolio customers also get their dashboards.

Billing questions

The small print, in plain English

How do I pay?

By debit or credit card, through Stripe. Stripe handles the payment securely and sends you a receipt. Presence Pro never sees or stores your card number.

Is VAT included?

Prices on this page are before VAT. VAT is added at checkout where it applies, and appears on your invoice.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Cancelling stops the next payment and your plan runs to the end of the period you've already paid for. There's no minimum term. Full terms are in our terms of service.

What's the difference between monthly and yearly?

Yearly billing is available on the Single hotel plan: it is paid once and costs less than twelve monthly payments, and the page shows exactly how much you save. Portfolio is billed monthly. Reports are the same either way.

How does Portfolio pricing work?

Portfolio is for groups with five or more hotels. You pay the same price for every hotel, every month, and you choose how many hotels when you subscribe. You get a portfolio dashboard with trends across all your hotels, and each hotel also gets its own dashboard that shows only that hotel. To add or remove hotels later, use the billing link in your Stripe receipt or email us.

Why does Portfolio cost more per hotel?

Because it includes more: a portfolio dashboard with trends across every hotel, ongoing analysis of which hotels need attention, actions tracked hotel by hotel, a separate dashboard for each hotel, and priority support. If you only want the monthly report for several hotels, Single hotel can be bought for as many hotels as you like.

Can I buy Single hotel for more than one hotel?

Yes. Choose how many hotels at checkout, and each gets its own monthly report. If you reach five hotels and want the portfolio dashboard and a dashboard for each hotel, email us and we'll move you to Portfolio.

Can I change plan?

Yes. Email us and we'll move you between Single hotel and Portfolio.

Will the reports guarantee AI recommends my hotel?

No, and no honest provider can promise that. The report shows what an AI assistant said, where it got its information and what your own pages leave unanswered, so you can fix real gaps. The AI answers are a labelled sample of one assistant on one day.

What if I want to see one first?

Read the sample report, or ask us any question about what your hotel's report would cover.

I need an invoice or have a billing problem

Stripe emails a receipt with every payment. For an invoice in your company's name or anything else about billing, email {{ contact.email }}.
