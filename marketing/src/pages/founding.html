How it works

By application, not automatic.

1. Apply

Tell us about your hotel below. We read every application, and we aim to reply within a few working days.

3. Subscribe with your code

If you're approved we email you a one-time code. At checkout you enter it and pay {{ price_founding }} a month (plus VAT where it applies). The offer ends by itself after {{ founding_months }} months.

After {{ founding_months }} months

Your subscription moves to the standard price in force at that time. We write to you well before it changes, and you can cancel instead; cancelling stops the next payment and your plan runs to the end of the period you've paid for. Founding terms are in the terms of service.
